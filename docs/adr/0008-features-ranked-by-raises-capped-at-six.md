# 8. An Area's Features are ranked by Raises and capped at six

## Decision

Each Area keeps one Features list in `CHARTER.md`, at most six long, shown as a Top 3 and a Bottom 3 and sorted by how often each Feature was raised (ties to the most recently raised). The retro counts Raises from `progress.txt`, at most one per Feature per Cycle, adds new ideas at 1×, removes the finished Feature, and drops the lowest when the list passes six. The human picks the next Feature at the grill, usually from the Top 3, but may pick any Feature or something new.

## Why

The list ranks itself from what keeps coming up, so nobody grooms it, it never rots, and a finished Cycle always leaves something to do without a Brainstorm.

## Cost

A good idea raised once can fall off the bottom and is gone until someone raises it again. A count says how often an idea came up, not how much it is worth.
